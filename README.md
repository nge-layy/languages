# 🇩🇪 German A1 to A2 Intensive Study Plan (3 Months / 12 Weeks)

> **Goal:** Progress from complete beginner (A1) to elementary proficiency (A2) in 12 weeks.  
> **Total Time Commitment:** ~150–180 hours (12–15 hours/week | ~2 hours/day).

## 🗓️ Phase 1: Level A1 (Weeks 1–6)

### Key Grammar Foundations
* **Gender & Articles:** *der* (masculine), *die* (feminine), *das* (neuter), *die* (plural).
* **Present Tense (*Präsens*):** Regular verb conjugations and key irregulars (*sein*, *haben*).
* **Cases:** Nominative (subject) and Accusative (direct object).
* **Verbs:** Separable verbs (*trennbare Verben*) & Modal verbs (*können, müssen, wollen, dürfen, sollen, mögen*).
* **Sentence Structure:** Main clause V2 rule (verb in 2nd position) & Negation (*nicht* vs. *kein*).

### Week-by-Week Breakdown

| Week | Grammar Focus | Core Vocabulary & Topics | Practical Skills |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Week 1** | Phonetics, V2 Word Order, Present Tense basics | Greetings, Alphabet, Numbers (1–100), Countries & Languages | Introduce yourself, spell your name, ask basic personal questions. |
| **Week 2** | Noun Genders, Definite/Indefinite Articles, Accusative Case | Family, Food & Drinks, Ordering at a Café/Restaurant | Order meals, express preferences, describe your family. |
| **Week 3** | Separable Verbs, Telling Time, Plural Nouns | Daily Routine, Days/Months, Hobbies & Free Time | Schedule appointments, talk about everyday activities. |
| **Week 4** | Modal Verbs (*können, müssen, etc.*), Negation (*nicht/kein*) | Directions, Shopping, Work & Professions | Ask for help, state rules/obligations, navigate a store. |
| **Week 5** | Possessive Articles (*mein, dein*), Accusative Prepositions | Home & Living, Furniture, City Locations | Describe your apartment, specify locations in a city. |
| **Week 6** | Intro to Conversational Past (*Perfekt*), Phase 1 Review | Travel, Transportation, Weather | Talk about recent activities and review A1 competencies. |

## 🗓️ Phase 2: Level A2 (Weeks 7–12)

### Key Grammar Foundations
* **Dative Case:** Indirect objects and strictly Dative prepositions (*aus, bei, mit, nach, seit, von, zu*).
* **Two-Way Prepositions (*Wechselpräpositionen*):** Location (Dative) vs. Direction (Accusative).
* **Past Tenses:** *Perfekt* (spoken past with *haben*/*sein*) & *Präteritum* (simple past for *war*/*hatte*).
* **Subordinate Clauses:** Word order shifts with *weil, dass, wenn, obwohl*.
* **Adjective Declension:** Endings after definite, indefinite, and zero articles.
* **Reflexive & Subjunctive II:** Reflexive verbs (*sich freuen*) and polite requests (*würde, könnte, hätte*).

### Week-by-Week Breakdown

| Week | Grammar Focus | Core Vocabulary & Topics | Practical Skills |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Week 7** | Dative Case deep-dive, Dative Prepositions | Shopping, Gifts, Giving Directions | Express ownership, give detailed directions, shop for gifts. |
| **Week 8** | Present Perfect (*Perfekt*) with *haben/sein* + Past Participles | Travel, Holidays, Past Experiences | Describe vacations, weekend activities, and past life events. |
| **Week 9** | Subordinate Clauses (*weil, dass, wenn, obwohl*) | Opinions, Health & Illness, Body Parts | Give reasons, express feelings, explain health issues to a doctor. |
| **Week 10** | Two-Way Prepositions (*Wechselpräpositionen*), Adjective Endings | Housing/Moving, Clothing, Weather | Describe room layouts, buy clothing with color/size details. |
| **Week 11** | Reflexive Verbs, Comparatives/Superlatives, Subjunctive II | Daily Routine (adv.), Making Plans, Politeness | Make polite requests, compare products, express wishes. |
| **Week 12** | Narrative Past (*Präteritum* basics), Comprehensive A2 Review | Media, Storytelling, Future Plans | Write short formal emails, hold longer conversations, final test. |
